Flood Risk Level Guide

What each status means & what action to take
SAFE
No Flood Risk
Rainfall within normal limits. Soil drainage and river levels stable. No inundation expected in this ward.
No action needed — monitor conditions normally.
WATCH
Elevated Monitoring
Rainfall rising above seasonal norms. Low-lying areas or wards near rivers may see surface waterlogging if rain continues.
Stay alert — avoid travel near streams or drains.
WARNING
High Flood Probability
Model predicts significant inundation risk. Drainage likely overwhelmed. Roads and ground-floor structures could flood within hours.
Take precautions — move vehicles, avoid flood-prone roads.
DANGER
Severe Inundation Imminent
Critical flood risk detected. Major inundation predicted. Life and property at immediate risk. Emergency response activation recommended.
Evacuate low-lying areas — follow NDRF / civic authority guidance.
Risk levels are AI/ML model predictions trained on synthetic rainfall + terrain data for Pune's 15 wards. Confidence scores (%) indicate model certainty. Approximate thresholds: Safe <15 mm/3h  ·  Watch 15–35 mm  ·  Warning 35–65 mm  ·  Danger >65 mm accumulated rainfall in the 3-hour window.

Project Report Summary

About Varshanetra

Team Vortex · Problem Statement SIH26071 · Theme: Disaster Management · Smart India Hackathon 2026

What it does: predicts short-horizon rainfall severity and ward-level flood risk for Pune from rainfall history and terrain, and surfaces which factors are driving each prediction.

Tech stack: Python, scikit-learn (Random Forest), gradient-boosted trees (XGBoost, or a sklearn fallback where unavailable), permutation-importance/SHAP-style explainability, FastAPI backend, PostgreSQL+PostGIS design target, React/Leaflet-class frontend.

Current status, plainly: trained and evaluated on a physically-motivated synthetic dataset, not yet on real IMD/MOSDAC history — that access is pending. Terrain values per ward are plausible placeholders, not a real DEM extract. Every view in this dashboard is running genuine model output; none of the numbers are hand-typed, but the data feeding the model is not yet the real thing.

This dashboard: replays a real trained model across a fixed demo window rather than a live feed, since a live MOSDAC/IMD connection isn't available yet. Swapping in a live feed later only touches one function — see the project README.
